# Claims and limitations

## Supported claims

- MobiOrigin had higher three-class macro-F1 than geNomad on the frozen prospective external cohort.
- MobiOrigin had higher plasmid binary F1 than geNomad on the frozen prospective external cohort.
- Both co-primary advantages were supported by paired source-level bootstrap intervals with Holm correction.
- MobiOrigin had higher plasmid sensitivity in descriptive aggregate analysis.

## Required qualifications

- MobiOrigin had lower plasmid precision than geNomad.
- MobiOrigin had lower prediction coverage because its frozen policy permits abstention.
- Precision and sensitivity were not preregistered co-primary superiority endpoints.

## Unsupported claims

- MobiOrigin has higher plasmid precision than geNomad.
- MobiOrigin has higher prediction coverage than geNomad.
- MobiOrigin is universally superior across all datasets, organisms, or use cases.
- The frozen external cohort may be used for retrospective tuning or error mining.
