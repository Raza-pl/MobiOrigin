# Real-assembly operational evidence: mandatory claim boundaries

- These two assembly subsets have no frozen record-level ground truth.
- Runtime, call fraction, coverage, and label-free agreement may be reported.
- Accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, predictive value, or superiority may not be inferred.
- Pairwise agreement is prevalence-dependent and does not validate either tool.
- Evidence tiers A–E prioritize follow-up; they are not clinical risk scores.
- ARG or mobility evidence can support biological plausibility but cannot by itself prove plasmid origin, host pathogenicity, transmissibility, or clinical hazard.
- The deterministic subsets characterize this workstation workflow and are not prevalence-representative cohorts.
