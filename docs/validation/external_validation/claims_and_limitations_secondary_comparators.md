# Secondary comparator claims and limitations

## Supported exploratory statements

- MobiOrigin had the highest plasmid-binary F1 and balanced accuracy among MobiOrigin, PlasClass, PlasFlow v1, PLASMe, and Platon on this frozen external cohort.
- MobiOrigin-minus-comparator paired differences were positive for both F1 and balanced accuracy against all four secondary tools, with all eight exploratory Holm-adjusted tests meeting the prespecified 0.05 threshold.

## Required trade-off disclosure

- PlasClass had slightly higher plasmid sensitivity than MobiOrigin.
- PLASMe and Platon had higher plasmid precision than MobiOrigin, accompanied by substantially lower sensitivity.
- PlasFlow v1 had lower prediction coverage than MobiOrigin.
- The secondary comparisons are post-hoc exploratory and must not be described as additional prospective co-primary evidence.

## Unsupported statements

- MobiOrigin has universally superior precision, sensitivity, or coverage.
- MobiOrigin is universally superior across datasets, organisms, sequence distributions, or operational settings.
- The external cohort can be reused for model, threshold, adapter, or semantic tuning.
